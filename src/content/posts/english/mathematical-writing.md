> 起步笔记：以下是练习材料，后续可替换为自己的精读和改写记录。

## 四个常用句式

| 目的 | 表达 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 提出假设 | Assume that … | Assume that the solution is smooth. |
| 引入对象 | Let … denote … | Let Ω denote a bounded domain. |
| 说明依据 | By …, we obtain … | By integration by parts, we obtain an energy identity. |
| 得出结论 | It follows that … | It follows that the norm is non-increasing. |

## 一段简短练习

Assume that the solution is smooth and satisfies homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions. Multiplying the equation by the solution and integrating over the domain, we obtain an energy identity. The boundary term vanishes. It follows that the L² norm is non-increasing.

这段话按“假设 → 操作 → 边界处理 → 结论”展开。改写时应核对逻辑关系，再检查语法和术语。
